# About

I am final year PhD student at **King's College London**, where I study the effects of climate change on the Antarctic ice sheet. I use numerical models and machine learning to explore the uncertainty in future sea level projections, so that we can better prepare for the effects of rising sea level on coastal communities.

## Research Interests
- Machine learning emulation
- Ice-ocean interactions
- Tipping elements in the climate system
